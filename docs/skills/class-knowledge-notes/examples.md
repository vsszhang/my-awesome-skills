# Class Knowledge Notes examples

## Transcript and slides

```text
Use $class-knowledge-notes to organize today's lecture. Use this transcript and the PDF in the course folder. Save a Markdown note in the course folder and put useful slide screenshots under artificial/<note-name>/.
```

## Recording and classroom photos

```text
Use $class-knowledge-notes on this recording and these board photos. Summarize the original language in Chinese, keep important Russian terms, and mark anything that is unclear in the audio.
```

Selected classroom photos are automatically corrected to a frontal view when photographed at an angle, then cropped to their relevant board or screen before insertion. Processed images are saved under `artificial/<note-file-stem>/` and checked for complete writing and diagrams without excessive stretching.

## Photographed screen and blackboard

```text
Use $class-knowledge-notes to organize these classroom photos. They contain a projected slide and blackboard derivations photographed from the side. Save the notes as 课堂知识点整理03.md and insert the relevant images beside the matching knowledge points.
```

The skill identifies both teaching surfaces, corrects each angled surface to a frontal view, crops away the classroom surroundings, and saves the results under `artificial/课堂知识点整理03/`. If a formula or diagram extends across boards, the processed images and explanation preserve that connection. Unreliable corner detection is reported instead of forcing a distorted result.

## Word output

```text
Use $class-knowledge-notes to organize these materials as a Word document. Embed the relevant slide images and include the source page numbers.
```
